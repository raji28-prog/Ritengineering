About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) was established in 2009 with a commitment to preparing young women for the challenges and opportunities of the modern digital landscape. Accredited by NBA and NAAC ('A' Grade), the department offers an approved annual intake of 180 seats in core CSE, with allied specializations in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (180 seats) and Data Science (60 seats).

Main Targets

  • Strong foundation in computing and programming.
  • Encouraging innovation and problem-solving skills.
  • Interdisciplinary learning with real-world relevance.
  • Preparing students for industry, higher education, and research.
  • Promoting ethics, teamwork, and leadership.

Future Applications

The department trains students for emerging domains like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Internet of Things, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, and Data Science, ensuring complete readiness for future enterprise needs.

Career Opportunities

Graduates pursue careers as Software Engineers, Data Scientists, Cloud Architects, Cybersecurity Specialists, AI Engineers, IT Consultants, Researchers, or Tech Entrepreneurs. Many also pursue MS degrees in top global universities.

Vision & Mission

Department Vision

"To evolve as a recognized center of academic excellence in Computer Science and Engineering, empowering women technocrats with innovative thinking, research aptitude, and ethical values to solve multifaceted societal challenges."

Department Mission

  • Deliver high-quality undergraduate and postgraduate education through outcome-based curricula and modern computational infrastructure.
  • Promote collaborative research, innovation, and entrepreneurship by establishing strong synergy with IT corporations.
  • Inculcate ethical mindset, lifelong learning habits, and social responsibility in women scholars.

Department Objectives & Work Culture

Core Educational Objectives

  • Provide profound theoretical foundation and hands-on skill in core programming paradigms and distributed systems.
  • Expose students to modern industrial software development lifecycles (Agile, DevOps, CI/CD, Microservices).
  • Nurture critical thinking through participation in hackathons, coding contests, and open-source contributions.
  • Foster interdisciplinary problem solving applying AI and Data Science across healthcare, education, and finance.

Collaborative Work Culture

The CSE department nurtures a vibrant, inclusive, and highly supportive work culture characterized by mutual respect, continuous faculty development, peer study circles, and proactive student mentorship. Faculty members maintain an open-door policy, encouraging students to bring innovative project concepts to fruition in our 24x7 incubator labs.

Course Outcomes (COs) & Program Outcomes (POs)

Program Outcomes (POs)

PO1 (Engineering Knowledge): Apply knowledge of mathematics, computing, and science to complex computer engineering problems.
PO2 (Problem Analysis): Identify, formulate, and analyze algorithmic requirements arriving at substantiated conclusions.
PO3 (Design & Development): Design solutions for complex computing problems meeting specified public health, safety, and environmental standards.
PO4 (Investigations): Conduct research-based investigations using synthesis of data to draw valid conclusions.
PO5 (Modern Tools): Create, select, and apply state-of-the-art software engineering techniques and modern IDEs.

PEOs & PSOs (Program Specific Outcomes)

PSO1 (Software Systems Architecture):

Ability to design, build, and deploy enterprise software systems using modern data structures, object-oriented design, and secure cloud microservices.

PSO2 (Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering):

Proficiency in applying machine learning, neural networks, and scalable data analytics to build intelligent automation and decision-support systems.

Project-Based Pedagogy

Every core programming subject incorporates a mandatory mini-project with mandatory GitHub version control and CI/CD testing.

Industry Immersion

Department of Electronics & Communication Engg

NBA Accredited • UGC Autonomous Division
B.Tech ECE • Approved Annual Intake: 60 Seats • Established: 2009

"From microchips that power modern spacecraft to 5G wireless networks connecting billions, ECE is the catalyst of modern physical computing. We train our engineers in VLSI design, embedded IoT architectures, and advanced RF communications."

ece.hod@rishimsengg.ac.in +91-967-623-3050 Room C-104, Academic Block C

About Electronics & Communication Engineering

Accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), the ECE Department blends electrical science, semiconductor physics, digital computing, and telecommunications. Students gain extensive experiential training in ASIC layout, FPGA synthesis, microcontrollers, and antenna design.

Key Targets

  • Solid foundation in analog & digital electronics.
  • VLSI design using Cadence & Mentor Graphics.
  • Embedded microcontrollers (ARM, Arduino, ESP32) for IoT.
  • Signal processing and RF telecommunication systems.

Vision & Mission

To empower women engineers in ECE through rigorous education, advanced circuit laboratories, and innovation in VLSI, embedded systems, and wireless networks.

Placements & Careers

Recruited by top semiconductor, telecom, and automotive electronics firms: Qualcomm, Texas Instruments, Bosch, Medha Servo Drives, Intel partners, Wipro, and TCS.

ECE Laboratories & Facilities

VLSI & Microelectronics CAD Lab

Industry-standard Electronic Design Automation (EDA) suites (Cadence, Xilinx Vivado, Mentor Graphics) for schematic entry, simulation, and FPGA implementation.

Embedded Systems & IoT Innovation Center

ARM Cortex-M development boards, Raspberry Pi 4 nodes, multi-sensor interface shields, Zigbee/LoRa wireless modules, and digital oscilloscopes.

Digital Signal Processing & Communications Lab

MATLAB, Simulink, Texas Instruments DSP starter kits, RF signal generators, spectrum analyzers, and software-defined radio (SDR) testbenches.

Microwave & Optical Communications Lab

Gunn diode microwave benches, slotted line waveguides, antenna radiation pattern measuring turntables, and fiber optic communication training kits.

Department of Humanities & Sciences (H&S)

First-Year Academic Foundation • Multi-Disciplinary Sciences
Mathematics • Applied Physics • Engineering Chemistry • English & Soft Skills

"The Humanities and Sciences department is the bedrock upon which world-class engineering is constructed. We cultivate mathematical rigor, experimental scientific inquiry, linguistic poise, and ethical grounding in every first-year engineering scholar."

hns.hod@rishimsengg.ac.in +91-967-623-3050 Room A-001, Main Administrative Block

About the Department of Humanities & Sciences

The Humanities and Sciences (H&S) Department serves as the academic foundation for all B.Tech first-year engineering students. Comprising Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English, the department bridges intermediate education with higher technological study, instilling mathematical dexterity, laboratory rigor, and sophisticated communication competence.

Engineering Mathematics

Linear algebra, multivariable calculus, vector calculus, differential equations, and numerical techniques forming the analytical foundation of computer science and electronics.

Applied Physics & Chemistry

Semiconductor physics, quantum optics, laser technology, battery electrochemistry, nanomaterials, and environmental conservation with hands-on lab experiments.

English & Communication

Computerized English Language and Communication Skills (ELCS) and Advanced Communication Skills (AECS) laboratories for phonetic training, interviews, and public speaking.

Humanities & Sciences Laboratories

English Language Communication Skills (ELCS) Lab

60 computerized language booths with interactive audio-visual software (K-Van, Rosetta Stone) for phonetics, neutral pronunciation, and mock interviews.

Engineering Physics Laboratory

Spectrometers, laser diffraction kits, ultrasonic interferometers, Hall effect apparatus, and Planck's constant experimental setups.

Engineering Chemistry & Environmental Lab

Digital pH meters, conductometers, colorimeters, flame photometers, and water hardness titration benches conforming to BIS analytical standards.

Basic Engineering Workshop & Fabrication Lab

Fittings, carpentry, sheet metal fabrication, house wiring, and welding safety demonstration bays for hands-on engineering literacy.
