RISHI M.S INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY FOR WOMEN
Code: RITW

Connect with RISHI M.S INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY FOR WOMEN (RITW) — a leading autonomous institution in Telangana dedicated to empowering women through world-class technical education, innovation, and holistic development. At RITW, academic excellence meets practical learning, fostering confident, industry-ready engineers equipped with strong values and leadership skills. With cutting-edge infrastructure, experienced faculty, and exceptional placement opportunities, RITW provides an inspiring platform for women to excel in engineering, research, and entrepreneurship. Join RITW to experience a transformative journey that shapes future innovators, achievers, and leaders of tomorrow.
