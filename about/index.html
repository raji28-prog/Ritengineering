Located on a 5.2-acre lush green campus opposite JNTU, Hyderabad, Telangana, RITW was founded in memory of the legendary singer Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi.

Foundational Legacy

In Revered Memory of Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi

A timeless symbol of dedication, humility, discipline, and excellence inspiring every student at RITW.

Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi
(1916–2004) • Cultural Icon & Humanitarian

RITW proudly carries the name of Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi, one of India's most celebrated Carnatic vocalists and a timeless symbol of dedication, humility, discipline, and excellence.

She was the first musician to receive the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour. Through her music and humanitarian work, she inspired millions across the world and became an enduring icon of Indian culture and values.

Inspired by her extraordinary legacy, RITW is committed to empowering young women with quality education, strong ethical values, leadership, innovation, and a lifelong passion for learning. Every student who walks through our campus is encouraged to pursue excellence with the same grace, determination, and compassion that defined her remarkable life.

The institute is approved by AICTE, recognized by the Government of Telangana, and affiliated with JNTUH. We are proud to be NAAC 'A' grade accredited, UGC Autonomous, NBA Accredited, and NIRF-listed, reflecting our commitment to quality education.

Our goal is to make learning a purposeful experience, nurturing independence, discipline, critical thinking, and creativity. We offer an inclusive and diverse academic environment where students are empowered to become future-ready professionals and socially conscious leaders.

Logo Description & Symbolism

The logo of Rishi MS Institute of Engineering and Technology for Women (RITW) is a visual expression of education as the foundation of women's empowerment. It portrays a woman rising gracefully from the pages of an open book, symbolizing knowledge as the force that uplifts and transforms. The flowing lines reflect growth, confidence, and determination, while the book represents the institute's strong academic base and commitment to learning.

Shades of purple and gold are used to signify wisdom, dignity, and excellence. Drawing inspiration from the ideals of Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi Garu, the design mirrors RITW's vision of shaping women who combine intellect with integrity, and who lead with both courage and compassion in every sphere of life.

About Rishi College Group of Institutions

Rishi Group of Colleges – Empowering Future Women Leaders under Rishi UBR Educational Society (Est. 1990).

A Beacon of Women's Education

Rishi MS Institute of Engineering and Technology for Women (RITW) stands as a beacon of excellence in women's education in the fields of engineering and technology. Established in 2009, RITW is a premier institution located in the lush green campus of 5.2 acres, opposite JNTU in Hyderabad, Telangana. The college, an integral part of the Rishi UBR Educational Society, is dedicated to creating an environment where women are encouraged to excel, innovate, and lead in the world of technology.

A Legacy of Excellence (Since 1990)

The foundation of the Rishi UBR Educational Society was laid in 1990 with the aim of empowering women through education. The society's journey began with the establishment of the Rishi UBR Degree College for Women in 1991, in memory of the revered Late Sri U.B. Raghavendra Rao Garu, a retired IAS officer and former Secretary to the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh.

Over the years, the society expanded into technical education with the establishment of RITW in 2009. Approved by AICTE and affiliated with JNTUH, RITW has made a significant mark in women's engineering education.

Core Ideals

Vision, Mission & Quality Policy

Exact official statements guiding pedagogical governance and student development at RITW.

VISION
"To be a center of excellence in producing women engineers and scientists who are professionally competent social leaders to face multi disciplinary global environment by imparting quality technical education, values and ethics through innovation methods of teaching and learning."
MISSION
  • To promote women technocrats capable enough to resolve the problems faced by the society using the knowledge imparted.
  • To prepare self-reliant women engineers for technological growth of the nation and society by laying strong theoretical foundation accompanied by wide practical training.
  • To equip the young women with creative thinking capabilities and empowering them towards innovation.
Quality Policy
  • To continuously strive for excellence in education by adopting innovative pedagogical tools to constantly foster development of the institution by updating the resources of the institution.
  • To build partnerships with industry and other organizations to empower the students.
Strategic Direction

Institutional Objectives

Prescribed educational objectives delivering student-centred curricula and industry partnerships.

  • To deliver high‑calibre technical education to every student admitted at RITW, nurturing a smart campus culture, innovation, and holistic growth.
  • To design and maintain student‑centred curricula that ensure every graduate emerges not only with technical competence, but also leadership, communication, business awareness, teamwork, and a deep sense of social accountability.
  • To enhance our teaching‑learning environment through skilled faculty, state‑of‑the‑art labs, and immersive hands‑on work—raising campus placement rates and strengthening partnerships with industry and research organizations.
  • To promote intellectual growth, critical thinking, and a research mindset among faculty and students, encouraging publications, patents, and collaborative projects.
  • To uphold high standards of governance, transparent processes, and sustainable campus practices, fostering an inclusive community dedicated to nation‑building.

Infrastructure Modernization
