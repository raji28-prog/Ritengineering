Established in 2009 in revered memory of legendary vocalist Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi. Approved by AICTE, affiliated with JNTU Hyderabad, and accredited by NAAC with 'A' Grade. Dedicated exclusively to moulding empowered women technologists, researchers, and global leaders on a vibrant 5.2-acre campus opposite JNTU, Hyderabad.

Foundational Inspiration

In Revered Memory of Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi

Dedicated to the timeless values of devotion, discipline, humility, and uncompromising excellence in service of humanity.

Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi
(1916–2004) • Cultural Icon & Humanitarian

RITW proudly carries the name of Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi, one of India's most celebrated classical vocalists and a timeless symbol of dedication, humility, and spiritual artistry. She was the first musician in Indian history to be honoured with the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.

Through her concerts and selfless philanthropy, she mobilized millions for public charities, hospitals, and educational trusts across the world. Inspired by her extraordinary legacy, RITW is committed to empowering young women with world-class technical education, ethical fortitude, leadership capabilities, and an enduring passion for societal transformation.

Sponsoring Body

About Rishi Group of Institutions

Operating under the aegis of the Rishi UBR Educational Society (Est. 1990) to pioneer exemplary professional education in Telangana.

Legacy of Rishi UBR Educational Society

Founded in 1990, the Rishi UBR Educational Society was established to commemorate the towering administrative vision of Late Sri U.B. Raghavendra Rao Garu (IAS Retd.), former Secretary to the Chief Minister. The society commenced its educational crusade with the establishment of Rishi UBR Degree College for Women in 1991, followed by RITW Engineering College in 2009.

Over the past three decades, the Rishi Group has evolved into a powerhouse of women's education, having nurtured more than 25,000 alumni across computer science, electronics, management, life sciences, and commerce.

Rishi Group Sister Institutions

  • Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) UGC Autonomous • NAAC 'A' • NBA Accredited • B.Tech in CSE, AI&ML, DS, IT, ECE
  • Rishi UBR Degree & PG College for Women (Est. 1991) Affiliated to Osmania University • B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, M.Sc, M.Com
  • Rishi Institute of Management & Research Post-graduate business analytics, executive development & corporate incubation
Core Pillars

Vision, Mission & Quality Policy

Guiding compass steering institutional pedagogy, industry collaboration, and graduate outcomes.

Our Vision
"To emerge as a premier centre of engineering education and research dedicated exclusively to women, fostering technological excellence, ethical leadership, innovation, and self-reliance to empower them as impactful catalysts in building a progressive and sustainable global society."
Our Mission
  • Deliver industry-aligned curriculum using outcome-based pedagogical methods and advanced computing laboratories.
  • Instil a deep culture of applied research, intellectual property generation, and entrepreneurship among women scholars.
  • Cultivate uncompromising professional ethics, environmental consciousness, and compassionate social empathy.
  • Forge robust strategic alliances with global tech corporations, premier research bodies, and premier universities.
Quality Policy
"RITW is resolutely committed to achieving sustained educational excellence by continuously reviewing and enhancing our teaching-learning frameworks, state-of-the-art infrastructural assets, faculty research competence, and transparent governance protocols, satisfying all statutory requirements of AICTE, UGC, NAAC, NBA, and JNTUH."

Statutory Framework

Governing Council & Academic Advisory Board

Governed in strict conformity with UGC Autonomous Regulations and JNTU Hyderabad statutory directives.

Statutory Councils

RITW's autonomous administrative architecture comprises four core statutory bodies:

  • 1. Governing Body (GB): Highest policymaking apex body overseeing institutional vision, budget, expansions, and compliance.
  • 2. Academic Council (AC): Reviews and ratifies syllabi, examination regulations, academic calendars, and grading schemes.
  • 3. Board of Studies (BOS): Departmental councils of faculty and university nominees formulating course curricula.
  • 4. Finance Committee: Audits institutional accounts, capital investments, fee structures, and scholarship allocations.
