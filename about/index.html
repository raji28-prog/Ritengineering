About Our College RITW

Established in 2009, Rishi MS Institute of Engineering and Technology for Women (RITW) is a leading institution dedicated exclusively to women's education in engineering and technology. Located on a 5.2-acre lush green campus opposite JNTU, Hyderabad, Telangana, RITW was founded in memory of the legendary singer Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi.

Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi (1916–2004)

In Revered Memory of Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi

RITW proudly carries the name of Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi, one of India's most celebrated Carnatic vocalists and a timeless symbol of dedication, humility, discipline, and excellence.

She was the first musician to receive the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour. Through her music and humanitarian work, she inspired millions across the world and became an enduring icon of Indian culture and values.

Inspired by her extraordinary legacy, RITW is committed to empowering young women with quality education, strong ethical values, leadership, innovation, and a lifelong passion for learning. Every student who walks through our campus is encouraged to pursue excellence with the same grace, determination, and compassion that defined her remarkable life.

The institute is approved by AICTE, recognized by the Government of Telangana, and affiliated with JNTUH. We are proud to be NAAC 'A' grade accredited, UGC Autonomous, NBA Accredited, and NIRF-listed, reflecting our commitment to quality education.

RITW is part of the Rishi UBR Educational Society, founded in 1990. The society's journey began with the establishment of Rishi UBR Degree College for Women in 1991, commemorating Late Sri U.B. Raghavendra Rao Garu, a retired IAS officer and former Secretary to the Chief Minister.

Our goal is to make learning a purposeful experience, nurturing independence, discipline, critical thinking, and creativity. We offer an inclusive and diverse academic environment where students are empowered to become future-ready professionals and socially conscious leaders.

Leadership

Prof. S. Jeelani – Chairman

A renowned academician with over 30 years of experience in teaching, research, and administration. He currently serves as Director of the Centre for Virtual Learning at the University of Hyderabad. He has authored 60+ research papers, published 3 books, and is a recipient of the prestigious "Best Citizen of India" award.

Ms. Rajasree – Secretary

A founding member of Rishi UBR Educational Institutions and a strong proponent of women's empowerment. She currently serves as Vice-President of the Shirdi Sai Baba Temple, Banjara Hills, and has been honored with the "Vikas Ratan Gold Medal Award" by the International Integration Society, New Delhi.

Why Choose RITW?

  • ✅ Women-centric institution committed to technical excellence and empowerment
  • ✅ Legacy of quality education under the Rishi UBR Educational Society
  • ✅ Accredited with NAAC 'A' grade, UGC Autonomous, NBA Accredited and listed under NIRF
  • ✅ Strategic location opposite JNTU-Hyderabad for academic collaboration and convenience
  • ✅ Focus on academic excellence, personal growth, and leadership development

At RITW, we believe education should not only equip students with knowledge, but also with the confidence, independence, and creativity to thrive in the real world. Our graduates emerge as competent professionals and responsible leaders who contribute meaningfully to society.
