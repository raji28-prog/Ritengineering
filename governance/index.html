STRATEGIC ROADMAP

Strategic Objectives & Long-Term Vision

The Institution Development Plan (IDP) of Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) outlines the strategic roadmap for academic excellence, infrastructure growth, research initiatives, and holistic student development. It reflects our commitment to quality technical education aligned with AICTE, JNTU-H, and UGC guidelines, while fostering innovation, entrepreneurship, and community engagement among women engineers.

The IDP serves as a guiding document for the institution's short-term and long-term goals, covering areas such as curriculum enhancement, faculty development, industry collaborations, infrastructure upgradation, and student welfare initiatives.
