The Free Aviation Pilot Training Program at Rishi MS

Proud Moment for Rishi MS!

Rishi MS is delighted to announce the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with INDIGO REACH exclusively for Women (Bharat Care NGO) to launch a Free Aviation Pilot Training Program for B.Tech III and IV Year students.

This unique initiative aims to provide aspiring students with valuable exposure to the AVIATION INDUSTRY and create pathways toward professional PILOT training and aviation careers.

The Free Aviation Pilot Training Program will commence in September 2026, offering eligible students an exceptional opportunity to build the knowledge and skills required for a successful future in aviation.

  • For B.Tech III & IV Year Students
  • Free Aviation Pilot Training Program
  • Exposure to the Aviation Industry
  • Pathway towards Pilot Training & Aviation Careers
Program Commences: September 2026

We extend our sincere gratitude to INDIGO REACH (Bharat Care NGO) for partnering with Rishi MS in empowering students through this prestigious initiative.

In Revered Memory of Bharat Ratna M.S. Subbulakshmi

RITW proudly carries the name of Bharat Ratna Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmi, one of India's most celebrated Carnatic vocalists and a timeless symbol of dedication, humility, discipline, and excellence.

She was the first musician to receive the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honour. Through her music and humanitarian work, she inspired millions across the world and became an enduring icon of Indian culture and values.

Inspired by her extraordinary legacy, RITW is committed to empowering young women with quality education, strong ethical values, leadership, innovation, and a lifelong passion for learning. Every student who walks through our campus is encouraged to pursue excellence with the same grace, determination, and compassion that defined her remarkable life.

About RISHI M.S - RITW

Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women was established in 2009 to perpetuate the memory of “BHARAT RATNA” Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmiji to emulate moral values and quality parameters in Technical Education. Rishi MS Engineering College is located near JNTUH metro station, KUKATPALLY, HYDERABAD.

The college has a strong TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL, with continuous activities to ensure best Technical and Soft skills to students for industry readiness. The students from all the branches are participating in INTERNSHIPS, HACKATHON, WORKSHOPS, and skill development workshops in updated Technologies from time to time. The college had MOU with CISCO and TASK is the best platform for updating skills with multi-level technical training programs. Placement cell has achieved 100% placements for IV year students in reputed companies.
