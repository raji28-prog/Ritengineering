Institutional Motto & Vision

The primary motto of Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women is to impart premium technical education with strong ethical foundations. Situated centrally near JNTU Metro, Nizampet Road, Kukatpally, Hyderabad, our institution offers an intellectually stimulating, safe, and progressive ecosystem for girl students to emerge as global leaders in tech industries, research labs, and entrepreneurship.

  • JNTU Hyderabad Affiliated: Degrees recognised globally for higher studies and international employment.

Admission Guidelines

Admission Procedure & Quota Allocations

Admissions to the four-year B.Tech degree programs are conducted strictly as per statutory Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TSCHE) and JNTUH norms.

Category - A (70% Seats)

Convener Quota (TS EAMCET)

Eligibility: Candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) examination conducted by the Board of Intermediate Education, Telangana/AP with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry as optional subjects, or equivalent recognized qualification.

Selection & Allotment: Allotted centrally by the Convener, TS EAMCET through official web counselling based on state rank and candidate preferences.

Select College Code: RITW during web counselling choice filling.
Category - B (30% Seats)

Management / NRI Quota

Eligibility: Open to candidates who have secured not less than 45% marks (40% in case of reserved categories) in Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry in the 10+2 qualifying examination. Both TS EAMCET / JEE Main rank holders and merit-based applicants are eligible.

Selection & Allotment: Filled transparently by college management on merit basis following official notification in leading newspapers and guidelines prescribed by TSCHE.

For direct Management seats contact: +91-967-623-3050.

S.No Quota Category Seat Share Admission Process & Authority
