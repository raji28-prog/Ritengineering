Why RITW is the Best Women's Engineering College in Hyderabad

Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) stands as one of the top women's engineering colleges in Hyderabad, offering a transformative academic experience rooted in excellence, innovation, and empowerment. Located in Hyderabad, Telangana, RITW is AICTE approved, JNTU-H affiliated, UGC Autonomous, and holds NBA Accreditation and NAAC A grade — a rare combination that reflects our unwavering commitment to quality education.

For students seeking CSE, IT, or ECE admissions in Hyderabad through TS EAMCET 2026, RITW offers an unmatched environment: world-class labs, experienced faculty, 100+ industry training programs, and a placement record of 4000+ on-campus placements — 300+ every year for 6 consecutive years. Our women-only campus ensures a safe, focused, and inspiring space for every student to thrive.

As the best engineering college for girls in Telangana, RITW has consistently produced engineers who lead in software, electronics, IT services, and research. With RITW admissions 2026–27 now open, this is your opportunity to join a legacy of excellence. Apply today and take the first step toward a future defined by knowledge, skill, and purpose.
