Admission Process – TG EAPCET 2026

Admissions to Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women follow transparent statutory guidelines governed by the Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) and JNTU Hyderabad.

70%

Direct 2nd Year (3-Year B.Tech)

Supernumerary seats allocated for Polytechnic Diploma holders and B.Sc. Mathematics graduates through TG ECET counseling for direct entry into the 3rd semester of B.Tech.

Appear in TG EAPCET 2026

Register and appear for the TG EAPCET examination conducted by JNTU Hyderabad on behalf of TGCHE, and secure a qualifying rank.

02

Certificate Verification (HLC)

Book an online slot and attend the designated Help Line Center (HLC) with original certificates for statutory document verification.

06

Report in person at the RITW Admissions Cell, Kukatpally with original certificates, allotment order, and joining report to finalize admission.

Statutory Regulations

Admission Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria

Mandatory criteria prescribed by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE), and Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH).

B.Tech 1st Year (4-Year Regular) Eligibility

  • Gender Requirement: Exclusively for female candidates.
  • Nationality & Domicile: Candidates must be Indian Nationals and satisfy Telangana Local / Non-Local status requirements under the Presidential Order.
  • Educational Qualification: Must have passed or appeared for the final year of Intermediate Examination (10+2 pattern) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) conducted by the Board of Intermediate Education, Telangana / Andhra Pradesh, or equivalent examination recognized by BIE/JNTUH.
  • Minimum Qualifying Marks: At least 45% marks in group subjects (MPC) for open category; at least 40% marks for reserved categories (SC, ST, BC).
  • Age Criteria: Candidates must have completed 16 years of age as of 31st December of the admission year. There is no upper age limit.

B.Tech 2nd Year (Lateral Entry) Eligibility

  • Qualifying Diploma: Must possess a 3-Year State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET) Diploma in Engineering / Technology, or a B.Sc. Degree with Mathematics.
  • Minimum Aggregate: At least 45% aggregate marks in the qualifying diploma/degree (40% for reserved category SC/ST/BC).
  • Counseling Exam: Must qualify with an eligible rank in TG ECET 2026.
  • Branch Allocation: Branch allotment mapped according to SBTET diploma specialization and TGCHE counseling matrix.
  • Direct Entry: Admitted straight into the 3rd semester (2nd year B.Tech) under autonomous curriculum.

Category-B (Management / NRI Quota) Norms

  • Transparent Merit: 30% of sanctioned intake is filled under Management / NRI quota strictly following TGCHE regulations.
  • Merit Hierarchy: Order of merit: JEE Main qualifying rank > TG EAPCET rank > Intermediate (10+2 MPC) marks percentage.
  • NRI / NRI-Sponsored: Candidates sponsored by NRI parents/guardians are eligible under the NRI quota as per regulatory norms.
  • State Ratification: The complete list of admitted Category-B candidates is submitted to TGCHE for statutory approval and ratification.
  • No Capitation: Zero donation or capitation fees. Official receipt issued for tuition fees.

Campus Discipline & Anti-Ragging Mandate

  • Mandatory Attendance: Minimum 75% attendance across all subjects each semester is compulsory to appear for end-semester autonomous examinations.
  • Zero Tolerance for Ragging: Strictly prohibited under the Telangana Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1997 and UGC Regulations.
  • Safe Campus: 24x7 CCTV surveillance, active Anti-Ragging Squad, and dedicated Women's Grievance Redressal Cell.
  • Code of Conduct: Students must abide by the dress code, academic integrity, and institutional ethics.
