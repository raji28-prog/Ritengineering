Campus Life & Infrastructure

At Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW), education extends far beyond classrooms. Situated on a lush 5.2-acre campus opposite JNTU College Metro Station, Kukatpally, RITW provides a secure, inspiring, and technologically advanced ecosystem for women engineers. From industry-grade computing laboratories and smart e-classrooms to extensive sports grounds, vibrant student chapters, and community outreach, life at RITW empowers scholars to lead with innovation, confidence, and sisterhood.

Physical Infrastructure

Academic Excellence through State-of-the-Art Facilities

At RITW, infrastructure is more than just physical buildings—it is the strong foundation that empowers students to dream big, learn with confidence, and perform at their best.

Kukatpally Campus

A Sustainable, Student-Centric Campus

Spacious lecture halls, naturally lit corridors, well-ventilated classrooms, and eco-friendly landscaping ensure that learning remains comfortable and engaging throughout the day. Sustainability is at the core of RITW's infrastructure planning. From rainwater harvesting systems and solar energy integration to safe RO water plants, our campus promotes an eco-conscious lifestyle.

  • 5.2-Acre Green Campus: Calm, peaceful sanctuary right in the heart of Hyderabad.
  • Naturally Ventilated Theatres: Ergonomic seating, smart lighting and acoustic insulation.
  • Eco-Conscious Landscaping: Zero single-use plastic zone with tree-lined corridors.
Academic Excellence

Empowering Women Engineers through World-Class Facilities

Every department is equipped with specialized laboratories featuring industry-grade equipment, simulation tools, and software platforms that bridge the gap between classroom knowledge and practical expertise. High-speed internet connectivity, smart projectors, and audio-visual aids enhance the teaching-learning process.

  • Industry-Grade Equipment: Texas Instruments, Cisco, Python, Xilinx, MATLAB & Cadence suites.
  • Dedicated Computing Clusters: Modern Dell & HP desktop workstations with gigabit switching.
  • Uncompromising Safety: 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric gateways, and resident faculty wardens.
Knowledge Repositories

Central, Conventional & Digital Libraries

A center for learning and intellectual growth spanning extensive printed volumes, international e-journal subscriptions, and high-speed digital research terminals.

Central Knowledge Hub

Central Library

The Central Library at RITW is a premier knowledge hub designed to cater to the diverse academic, research, and general reading needs of students and faculty. Spanning a vast, well-lit, and quiet area, the library offers a rich repository of printed volumes, national and international journals, periodicals, and e-learning resources.

  • Web-Based OPAC: Fully computerized catalog for rapid book search and reservation.
  • Open-Access Shelving: Students can freely browse reference and lending stacks.
  • 200+ Seating Capacity: Spacious, naturally illuminated reading halls.
  • Reprographic Services: On-demand photocopier, digital scanner, and document printer.
Print Archives

Conventional Library

A repository of wisdom and tradition, housing extensive print collections across Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication, and Humanities & Sciences.

  • Over 25,000+ Printed Books: Standard textbooks, reference handbooks, and monographs.
  • University Question Paper Bank: Previous years' JNTUH and autonomous model question archives.
  • Project Dissertation Vault: Bound final-year B.Tech project reports and research monographs.
  • News & Periodicals Desk: Leading national dailies, business weeklies, and tech magazines.
Digital E-Resources

Digital Library

Equipped with modern multimedia computing terminals connected to a high-speed dedicated 1 Gbps lease line, providing 24/7 access to world-class online databases, e-books, and video lectures.

  • IEEE Xplore Access: Thousands of peer-reviewed journal papers and conference proceedings.
  • DELNET & NDL: Inter-library loan system and National Digital Library of India integration.
  • NPTEL / SWAYAM Video Repository: Terabytes of IIT & IISc recorded video courses.
  • Dedicated Workstations: Individual multimedia PCs with high-speed internet.

Smart Learning & AI-Driven Computing Centers

Interactive Theatres

E-Class Rooms

RITW's e-classrooms integrate high-definition interactive projectors, audio-visual reinforcement systems, smart boards, and digital lecterns. This technology-integrated pedagogy enables faculty to illustrate complex algorithmic concepts, circuit simulations, and structural architectures dynamically.

  • Interactive Smart Boards: Real-time digital whiteboarding and lecture recording.
  • Acoustic Speech Reinforcement: Collar mics, podium speakers, and clear audio delivery.
  • Ergonomic Tiered Seating: Unhindered line-of-sight for every student scholar.
Advanced Research Lab

AIML & Data Science Labs

Equipped with multi-core GPU computing workstations, high-speed switching, and industry-standard machine learning stacks. Scholars work on generative AI, neural networks, predictive analytics, and computer vision projects with hands-on mentoring.

  • High-Performance Workstations: Dedicated multi-core systems with GPU acceleration.
  • Software Environments: Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, R, OpenCV, Scikit-learn, and SQL.
  • Project Incubation: Real-time prototyping for hackathons and sponsored research.

National Pride & Military Training

National Cadet Corps (NCC Wing)

"Unity & Discipline" — Developing character, courage, comradeship, and selfless service among young women through structured military-style training and civic engagement.

NCC: Shaping Courage & Leadership

The National Cadet Corps unit at RITW reflects the institution's commitment to developing discipline, character, leadership, and patriotism among young women. The unit provides a platform where students grow into confident, responsible, and service-oriented citizens through structured training.

  • Drill Precision & Parades: Ceremonial guard-of-honour and weapon training.
  • Annual Training Camps (CATC): Intensive 10-day physical and tactical camps.
  • Adventure & Trekking: Rock climbing, obstacle courses, and disaster management drills.
  • National Day Honors: Guard displays on Independence Day and Republic Day.

Impact on Cadets & Armed Forces Careers

NCC training instills time management, mental fortitude, and teamwork. Cadets earning 'B' and 'C' certificates qualify for special preference quotas in defense forces, SSB interviews, police services, and public sector employment.

'B' & 'C' Certificate Benefits

NCC cadets gain direct entry to Service Selection Board (SSB) interviews for officer commissions in the Indian Army, Air Force, and Navy, without appearing for the CDS written examination.

Statutory Regulations

Admission Guidelines & Eligibility Criteria

Mandatory criteria prescribed by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Telangana State Council of Higher Education (TGCHE), and Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH).

B.Tech 1st Year (4-Year Regular) Eligibility

  • Gender Requirement: Exclusively for female candidates.
  • Nationality & Domicile: Candidates must be Indian Nationals and satisfy Telangana Local / Non-Local status requirements under the Presidential Order.
  • Educational Qualification: Must have passed or appeared for the final year of Intermediate Examination (10+2 pattern) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) conducted by the Board of Intermediate Education, Telangana / Andhra Pradesh, or equivalent examination recognized by BIE/JNTUH.
  • Minimum Qualifying Marks: At least 45% marks in group subjects (MPC) for open category; at least 40% marks for reserved categories (SC, ST, BC).
  • Age Criteria: Candidates must have completed 16 years of age as of 31st December of the admission year. There is no upper age limit.

B.Tech 2nd Year (Lateral Entry) Eligibility

  • Qualifying Diploma: Must possess a 3-Year State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET) Diploma in Engineering / Technology, or a B.Sc. Degree with Mathematics.
  • Minimum Aggregate: At least 45% aggregate marks in the qualifying diploma/degree (40% for reserved category SC/ST/BC).
  • Counseling Exam: Must qualify with an eligible rank in TG ECET 2026.
  • Branch Allocation: Branch allotment mapped according to SBTET diploma specialization and TGCHE counseling matrix.
  • Direct Entry: Admitted straight into the 3rd semester (2nd year B.Tech) under autonomous curriculum.

Category-B (Management / NRI Quota) Norms

  • Transparent Merit: 30% of sanctioned intake is filled under Management / NRI quota strictly following TGCHE regulations.
  • Merit Hierarchy: Order of merit: JEE Main qualifying rank > TG EAPCET rank > Intermediate (10+2 MPC) marks percentage.
  • NRI / NRI-Sponsored: Candidates sponsored by NRI parents/guardians are eligible under the NRI quota as per regulatory norms.
  • State Ratification: The complete list of admitted Category-B candidates is submitted to TGCHE for statutory approval and ratification.
  • No Capitation: Zero donation or capitation fees. Official receipt issued for tuition fees.

Campus Discipline & Anti-Ragging Mandate

  • Mandatory Attendance: Minimum 75% attendance across all subjects each semester is compulsory to appear for end-semester autonomous examinations.
  • Zero Tolerance for Ragging: Strictly prohibited under the Telangana Prohibition of Ragging Act, 1997 and UGC Regulations.
  • Safe Campus: 24x7 CCTV surveillance, active Anti-Ragging Squad, and dedicated Women's Grievance Redressal Cell.
  • Code of Conduct: Students must abide by the dress code, academic integrity, and institutional ethics.
