Institutional Grade Sheet & Evaluation

The National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) has conferred the institutional grade of 'A' to Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women in recognition of its sustained excellence across curricular design, pedagogy, student support services, research output, and state-of-the-art campus infrastructure.

7-Criteria Quality Performance:
  • Criterion 1: Curricular Aspects — Outcome-Based Education (OBE) and dynamic autonomous syllabi.
  • Criterion 2: Teaching-Learning and Evaluation — Experiential learning, high faculty cadre ratio, continuous evaluation.
  • Criterion 3: Research, Innovations & Extension — Active IPR filings, MSME Incubation, funded student projects.
  • Criterion 4: Infrastructure & Learning Resources — 1 Gbps internet, digital library (DELNET, NDLI), modern GPU labs.
  • Criterion 5: Student Support & Progression — 92.5% placements, Internshala Southzone Rank 5, merit aid.
  • Criterion 6: Governance, Leadership & Management — Transparent statutory councils, audit disclosures.
  • Criterion 7: Institutional Values & Best Practices — Solar campus, rainwater harvesting, women empowerment.

Policy Statement

IQAC Vision, Mission & Policy

Vision: To ensure quality culture as the prime institutional objective of Rishi M.S Institute through conscious, consistent, and catalytic measures for holistic student performance and faculty development.

Mission: To develop a deliberate system for conscious, consistent and catalytic improvement in the performance of the institution; and to channel the efforts of all stakeholders towards academic and research excellence.

Objectives: Systematize academic planning, promote ICT-enabled teaching pedagogies, establish robust industry feedback loops, and enforce comprehensive institutional audit frameworks.
