Placement Cell Vision & Methodology

The Training & Placement Cell at Rishi M.S Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women functions as a vital bridge between rigorous academia and real-world corporate expectations. The cell is committed to providing 100% placement assistance, organizing rigorous Campus Recruitment Training (CRT), industry hackathons, corporate expert lectures, and on-campus interview drives.

Technical & DSA Mastery

Intensive coding tracks in C, Python, Java, Data Structures, and System Design with 24/7 coding contest portals.

Aptitude & Reasoning

Daily practice sessions covering Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning, and Verbal Ability calibrated to AMCAT, CoCubes, and TCS NQT.

Soft Skills & Mock PIs

Professional communication, resume building workshops, group discussions, and mock technical & HR interviews by senior industry leaders.

Honeywell Center of Excellence
