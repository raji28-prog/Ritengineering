Placements - Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women

Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) has a robust Training & Placement Cell that ensures students are industry-ready. We provide continuous training in both technical and soft skills, ensuring that every student is well-prepared for their future careers. Our students actively participate in Internships, Hackathons, Workshops, and skill development sessions that focus on emerging technologies.

Our collaborations with industry leaders like CISCO and our tie-up with TASK have allowed us to offer multi-level technical training programs. These programs are designed to keep our students up-to-date with the latest trends in the tech industry, preparing them to meet the evolving needs of employers.

At RITW, we are proud to say that our Placement Cell has consistently achieved 100% placement rates for our final-year students in top-tier, reputed companies. The Training and Placement Cell works tirelessly, not only to arrange campus placements but also to provide essential pre-placement training, personality development sessions, and career counseling, ensuring the overall success of our students.

Our students’ consistent success in the job market is a testament to our commitment to excellence in technical education and skill development.

Training & Placement Cell - RITW | Industry-Ready Students & 100% Placement Record

Established in 2009, Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) is dedicated to providing high-quality technical education that reflects the moral values and aspirations of Smt. M.S. Subbulakshmiji, a Bharat Ratna recipient. Our institution is approved by AICTE, recognized by the Government of Telangana, and affiliated with JNTUH (Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University, Hyderabad).
