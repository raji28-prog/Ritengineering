About Training & Placement Cell At RITW

A proactive corporate interfacing department committed to bridging academia and industry with 100% career readiness.

Mission & Strategic Vision

Rishi MS Institute of Engineering & Technology for Women (RITW) has a robust Training & Placement Cell that ensures students are industry-ready from their second year of engineering. We provide continuous structured training in both deep technical proficiencies and soft executive competencies, ensuring that every woman engineer is well-prepared for prestigious corporate careers.

Our students actively participate in Corporate Internships, National Hackathons, Open-Source Bootcamps, and Hands-on Workshops focusing on Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Architectures, Full-Stack Systems, and Embedded IoT.

Industry Alliances & Training Tie-ups

Our strategic collaborations with industry giants like CISCO Networking Academy, our Center of Excellence with Honeywell, our cloud skilling partnership with SAP Code Unnati, and state government tie-up with TASK (Telangana Academy for Skill and Knowledge) allow us to deliver verified multi-level technical certifications.

These programs are meticulously designed to keep our students ahead of rapidly evolving technological demands, enabling consistent high-package selections across global IT and product engineering firms.

Skill Progression

Pre-Placement Training & 4-Year Roadmap

Structured semester-by-semester technical, analytical, and professional personality conditioning.

Y1

Year 1: Foundations & Computational Thinking

Semesters I & II

Fundamental algorithmic logic with C and Python, basic mathematical aptitude, English verbal fluency labs, and soft communication sessions to build unwavering stage confidence.

Y2

Year 2: Data Structures & Competitive Coding

Semesters III & IV

Intensive problem-solving on LeetCode/HackerRank, Object-Oriented Java/C++, Web frameworks, and participation in smart campus hackathons and product ideathons.

Y3

Year 3: Advanced Technologies & Industry CoE

Semesters V & VI

Enterprise skilling through SAP Code Unnati, Honeywell IoT lab, Full-stack system development, cloud deployments, and mandatory 6-8 week summer corporate internships with industry stipend opportunities.

Y4

Year 4: Intensive CRT & On-Campus Drives

Semesters VII & VIII

Company-specific assessment bootcamps (TCS NQT, Cognizant GenC, Accenture, Amazon), simulated mock technical/HR interviews conducted by senior corporate alumni, and direct recruitment clearances.

Corporate Labs

Centers of Excellence (CoE) Programs

SAP Code Unnati Center of Excellence

Cloud & Enterprise Systems

A premier initiative providing specialized credentials in SAP S/4HANA, ABAP programming, machine learning, and cloud enterprise services. Students build certified industry capstones with direct placement advantages across SAP partner ecosystems.

Honeywell Center of Excellence

Automation & Industrial IoT

Dedicated lab equipped with industrial sensor benches, automation programmable logic controllers, and embedded communication test suites. Mentored by Honeywell engineers to prepare women technocrats for core automation and cyber-physical engineering.
